Checked on 2026-10-09.

**How the checks were made.** This build environment's network policy blocks direct connections to census.gov, dor.wa.gov and intercitytransit.com (DNS fails). Every claim was therefore checked through web search against the official pages and documents listed below. The figures match the values in the brief. Before publishing, open the three links in a normal browser for a final click-through.

## Tax claims

| Claim (on screen or spoken) | Status | Source |

| "Washington has no broad state personal income tax on wages" (also "no state income tax on wages"; on screen `STATE INCOME TAX ON WAGES … $0 / NONE`) | Correct. Washington does not tax wages or salaries. Rates can differ by address, and the DOR lookup tool is authoritative. | [DOR notice Q3 2026, City of Olympia (PDF)](https://dor.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/Q326_Olympia_LLEP_MVET.pdf) · [DOR Q3 2026 LLEP notice (PDF)](https://dor.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/Q326_Olympia_LLEP.pdf) · [DOR sales and use tax rates](https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/sales-use-tax-rates) |

## Verified but not used

These figures were confirmed on the same QuickFacts page but left out of the video to keep it focused:

- Median household income: $81,302, in 2024 dollars.
- Median selected monthly owner costs with a mortgage: $2,261. Without a mortgage: $824.
- Mean travel time to work: 20 minutes.
- Population: 56,724 (estimate, July 1, 2025).

## Deliberately left out (rule 5 of the brief)

- **No cost-of-living index.** Third-party indexes use differing methodologies, so no "X% above the US average" claim appears.
- **No grocery, restaurant, coffee, utility, childcare or salary figures, and no monthly budget total.** None could be tied to a defensible current source.
- **No housing trend line.** The summary uses a static underline, not a chart, so no trend is implied.
